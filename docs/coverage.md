# Search coverage and limits

[Home](../README.md) · [Current review](full-review.md) · [Source/year inventory](../data/venue_search_inventory.csv) · [Related-survey comparison](../data/related_survey_comparison.csv)

The target window is 2022–2026. Broad source enumeration was recorded on 30 October 2026. Selected primary-source follow-up continued on 31 October 2026. This is a bounded literature search, not a certification that every CCF paper in every year has been assessed.

Conference year, first-online date and proceedings imprint year are separate. For example, DynamicVAR's retained citation uses the 2027 imprint, with a verified 2026-07-20 first-online date and ICIC 2026 conference year. A year-navigation heading is not a claim that the study was first published in that year.

## Current admission state

The 186 original records have 103 core, 68 background and 15 pending decisions. The follow-up adds 46 formally published records with 38 core and eight pending decisions. The 232-record catalog therefore contains 141 core papers. Three pre-2022 core studies are historical foundations, not part of the five-year search result.

Core admission requires an AR visual-generation object and original-source inference-efficiency evidence. Training results and token length alone do not qualify. Image, video and 3D generation remain separate. Model-evaluation budgets and memory savings are not relabeled complete image-generation latency. See [inclusion criteria](inclusion-criteria.md) and [paper-specific source notes](../papers/paper-notes.md).

## Publication policy

The main filter is formal publication at a CCF-listed venue. TMLR is explicitly allowed and is not claimed to be CCF-listed. Workshop-only papers are excluded. Findings is a separate track. ISSCC and IISWC records do not enter the strict core without established venue eligibility. A conference program or acceptance page alone does not resolve the final publication track.

CCF name checks used a 72-page mirrored 2026 directory because the official host could not be retrieved. Its equivalence to an official-host copy remains unverified. No new CCF grade is asserted from a similarly abbreviated venue.

Old under-review PDFs are checked against later primary records. Original PDFs and formal metadata remain separate. Full proof accessibility and performance-table reading are also distinct from publication confirmation.

## Outstanding candidates and limits

The catalog has 23 pending records. Another [15 earlier search candidates](../data/unresolved_search_candidates.csv) have not been added. Candidate lists and [visual follow-up decisions](../data/visual_candidate_screening.csv) retain the reason for each unresolved admission. Official abstracts support three current core records without a checked full performance table. No such record is described as a proof-verified or reproduced result.

The remaining source/year gaps prevent a claim of exhaustive five-year CCF coverage. Future corrections should append dated source evidence and update the per-paper decision. They should not retroactively describe failed retrievals as complete searches.
