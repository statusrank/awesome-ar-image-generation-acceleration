Core admission means that the recorded original-source passages identify an AR visual-generation object and an inference-efficiency result. It does not certify a proof or reproduce an experiment. Efficient new generators are distinguished from execution changes to existing models. Search-budget results have a separate baseline.

Image, video and 3D results are not pooled. Historical papers before 2022 remain labeled as such.
